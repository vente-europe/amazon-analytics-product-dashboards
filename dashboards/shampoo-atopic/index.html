Shampoo — Atopic — EU Market Topline

How this market is sized — claim-segmented, ASIN-level. Each market's Helium 10 X-Ray exports were merged and de-duplicated by ASIN, then every surviving listing's title + bullet copy was read (via the Amazon product API) to keep only genuine shampoos whose claims address sensitive / dry / irritated / itchy / atopic / eczema / psoriasis / seborrhoeic scalp — the Irritated / Atopic segment. Conditioners, masks, serums, 2-in-1s, sets, pet shampoos and purely cosmetic shampoos were excluded. Market size = sum of each kept ASIN's revenue, as a 12-month projection (30-day snapshot × 12). All figures in €; UK £ converted to € at 1.16117 (mid-market, 2026-06-30). PL: Rynek liczony na poziomie ASIN — tylko szampony (po przeczytaniu tytułu i bulletów przez API), których obietnice dotyczą skóry wrażliwej/atopowej/podrażnionej. Projekcja 12M = snapshot 30 dni × 12. Wszystko w €; UK £→€ po kursie 1.16117.
